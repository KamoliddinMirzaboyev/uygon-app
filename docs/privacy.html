Uyg'on — Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 12-oktabr

Qisqacha

Uyg'on hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumotni yig'maydi, yubormaydi va sotmaydi. Ilovada internet ruxsati yo'q, reklama yo'q, hisob ochish shart emas, uchinchi tomon tahlil (analytics) xizmatlari yo'q. Barcha ma'lumotlar faqat sizning telefoningizda saqlanadi.

Telefoningizda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

  • Budilniklar: vaqt, nom, takrorlanish kunlari, tanlangan ovoz va vazifa sozlamalari.
  • Ilova sozlamalari (til, standart vazifa va hokazo).
  • Agar siz o'zingiz tanlasangiz: budilnik ovozi sifatida tanlangan audio fayl nusxasi va QR/shtrix kod vazifasi uchun saqlangan kod matni.

Bu ma'lumotlar ilovaning shaxsiy xotirasida turadi, hech qayerga yuborilmaydi va ilova o'chirilganda yo'qoladi. Zaxira nusxa (backup) o'chirilgan.

Ruxsatlar va ulardan foydalanish

  • Bildirishnomalar, to'liq ekran bildirishnoma, aniq budilnik, qayta yoqilganda ishga tushish, wake lock, tebranish, fon xizmati: budilnik o'z vaqtida jaranglashi, ekran yonishi va ovoz o'ynashi uchun.
  • Kamera: faqat siz QR yoki shtrix kod vazifasini tanlaganingizda, kodni skanerlash uchun. Rasm yoki video saqlanmaydi va yuborilmaydi; tasvir telefonning o'zida qayta ishlanadi.
  • Jismoniy faollik: faqat "Yurish" vazifasida qadamlarni sanash uchun. Ma'lumot telefonda qoladi.
  • Harakat sensorlari: "Silkitish" va "Yurish" vazifalarida harakatni aniqlash uchun.

Uchinchi tomonlar

Ilova ma'lumotni hech kimga bermaydi. QR/shtrix kodni aniqlash Google ML Kit kutubxonasining telefon ichidagi modeli yordamida amalga oshadi; ilovada internet ruxsati bo'lmagani uchun hech qanday ma'lumot tarmoqqa chiqmaydi.

Bolalar

Ilova bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan va ular haqida ma'lumot yig'maydi.

O'zgarishlar

Uyg'on — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Summary

Uyg'on does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no ads, no account and no third-party analytics. All data stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Alarms: time, label, repeat days, chosen sound and task settings.
  • App settings (language, defaults).
  • Only if you choose them: a copy of an audio file used as an alarm sound, and the text of the QR/barcode saved for the scan task.

This data lives in the app's private storage, is never sent anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Cloud backup is disabled.

Permissions and how they are used

  • Notifications, full-screen notification, exact alarms, boot completed, wake lock, vibration, foreground service: so the alarm rings on time, turns the screen on and plays sound.
  • Camera: only when you pick the QR or barcode task, to scan the code. No photo or video is stored or sent; images are processed on the device.
  • Physical activity: only for the "Walk" task to count steps. Data stays on the device.
  • Motion sensors: to detect movement in the "Shake" and "Walk" tasks.

Third parties

The app shares nothing with anyone. Code recognition uses the on-device Google ML Kit model; because the app has no internet permission, no data leaves the phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about them.
