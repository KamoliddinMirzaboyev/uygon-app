## Title (23/30)
Uyg'on: Challenge Alarm

## Short description (75/80)
Alarm clock that only stops when you solve a task: math, QR, shake or walk.

## Full description (1440/4000)
Uyg'on is the alarm clock that won't let you fall back asleep.

Tired of tapping "dismiss" and sleeping through your morning? Uyg'on only turns the alarm off after you complete a task, so you are really awake.

⏰ RELIABLE
• Rings when the phone is locked, the app is closed, or after a reboot
• Turns the screen on and shows full screen
• One-time, daily or custom weekdays
• Unlimited alarms, each with its own label, sound and task

✅ 6 TASKS
• Math — solve easy, medium or hard problems 3 times in a row
• Shake — shake the phone 20–50 times
• Typing — type a motivational sentence (Uzbek and English)
• QR code — scan a QR code you chose (e.g. a sticker in the bathroom)
• Barcode — scan the barcode on a shampoo bottle or tea box
• Walk — walk 20–100 metres to stop the alarm

🔇 QUIET WHILE YOU WORK
Silence the sound for 1 or 2 minutes while you solve the task. If you don't finish, the music starts again.

😴 SMART SNOOZE
Choose the snooze length (5 or 10 min) and the maximum number of snoozes, or turn it off completely.

🌅 WAKE-UP CHECK
A few minutes after you dismiss it, the alarm rings again to confirm you are still up.

🎨 CLEAN AND SIMPLE
• Clean dark interface inspired by the iPhone Clock
• Smooth animations
• Fully in Uzbek (English available)
• A satisfying "tick" sound and haptics when setting the time

🔒 PRIVATE
No internet access, no ads, no account. Everything stays on your phone.

Start every morning with a win.
